# Introduction

Project Hail Mary, now a major motion picture starring Ryan Gosling, follows Ryland Grace on his suicide mission to save Earth from a dimming sun.

# Humor

The book's tone is very humorous, which makes this a very enjoyable book. If you enjoyed The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, then you are sure to love this. If you did not like it, you still might like this book as it is not as science fiction-y despite being very science fiction, and I think Andy Weir's dry sense of humor really helps this. It's a very accessible book even for non-sci-fi readers.

# Digestible Science

I am no rocket scientist, so I can't speak to how realistic this book is. However, throughout the book there is a lot of science and math, and it seems realistic to me anyway. I was pleased and unsurprised to find out that Andy Weir is a computer scientist (or was) with a passion for theoretical physics. His knowledge and writing skills make the science in the book very digestible. Perhaps not understandable, but palatable enough to enjoy it. This aids in making the book seem believable.

# Cadence

The book has a nice rhythm to it. One thing I always miss from non-crime fiction or thrillers is that sense of racing to find out what happens next. Project Hail Mary does a good job of keeping you on the edge of your seat.

# Character

Ryland Grace—played by Ryan Gosling in the new major motion picture—is a very funny and witty character. He seems real and genuine, and Weir's humor really suits this character. As this book is now a major motion picture starring Ryan Gosling, I had seen the posters before reading the book, which meant that I had an image of Ryland Grace prior to the movie. I feel the image suited and took nothing away from the book but was interesting to have the image decided for me.

# Ending

Without spoiling it, the ending for me was okay. It was a natural conclusion to an otherwise very good book, so I wasn't too disappointed. In my opinion, it's better to finish smart than get cocky and throw a Hail Mary (see what I did there).

# Rating

10
